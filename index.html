01THE STORY

A BOOKSHOP, BUILT OVER TIME

Some places
become part of
a town's memory.

B

Banamali Book Store was established in 1937, making it one of the oldest book stores in Jagatsinghpur. Its story is less about a grand building and more about a simple habit that lasts: walking in, looking around, and finding something worth taking home.

Today, the shop carries that same sense of familiarity — a place where learning, school life and everyday reading meet.

“A good bookshop doesn't just sell books. It keeps a town reading.”
